Eco-Conscious Operations

Environmental Responsibility

We adhere to ISO 14001:2015 frameworks, implementing strict containment systems at all terminal transfer points, vapor recovery units, and proactive spill-prevention protocols. We support Indonesia's 2060 Net Zero Emission roadmap through cleaner burning fuel alternatives.

Zero Marine Spill Protocol
Zero Harm Philosophy

Operational Safety (HSSE)

Safety is ingrained into our corporate DNA. Every marine voyage, road tanker trip, and fueling operation is governed by comprehensive Job Safety Analyses (JSA), certified personal protective equipment (PPE), and continuous personnel safety training.

5 Million+ Safe Man-Hours
